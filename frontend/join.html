      How do I know it's working?

      MeshMapper sends a batch every fifteen to thirty seconds while a wardriving session is running. Nothing is sent between sessions.

      Squares are only claimed where your radio hears a repeater. Driving through an area with no mesh coverage logs your position and claims nothing — that is normal, not a fault.

      To check, paste your API key into the setup check at the bottom of this page. It will tell you when MeshWars last heard from you and what is wrong if anything is.

      Lost your key? It's still sitting in MeshMapper — open Settings, then API Endpoints, and read it back out of the API Key field.

      Nothing is pushed to MeshWars at all. MeshWars polls meshview every 45 seconds and picks you up whenever your node broadcasts a position that a feeder hears — there is no session to start or batch to send.

      Squares are only claimed where a feeder hears your node's position. If you're somewhere with no feeder coverage, your position is never reported to meshview in the first place, so nothing gets claimed — that is normal, not a fault.

      To check, paste your API key into the setup check at the bottom of this page. It will tell you when MeshWars last picked up your node from meshview, how many squares you hold, and what is wrong if anything is.

      Lost your key? Nothing on a Meshtastic radio or in the Meshtastic app ever stores it — ask an operator for a new one.
