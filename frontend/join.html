      How do I know it's working?

      MeshMapper sends a batch every fifteen to thirty seconds while a wardriving session is running. Nothing is sent between sessions.

      Squares are only claimed where your radio hears a repeater. Driving through an area with no mesh coverage logs your position and claims nothing — that is normal, not a fault.

      To check, paste your API key into the setup check at the bottom of this page. It will tell you when MeshWars last heard from you and what is wrong if anything is.

      Lost your key? It's still sitting in MeshMapper — open Settings, then API Endpoints, and read it back out of the API Key field.

      Nothing is pushed to MeshWars at all. MeshWars reads coverage from FreqMapper and picks up your node whenever it's heard somewhere FreqMapper covers — there is no session to start or batch to send.

      Squares are only claimed inside that coverage. Outside it, a correctly configured radio paints nothing and nothing will tell you why — that's a coverage gap, not a fault, and it's the first thing to check if you've followed every setup step and still see no movement.
