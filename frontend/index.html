MeshWars

Teams claim 300 metre squares by reaching the mesh from them.

What the game is

Players are on one of seven teams. The map is divided into squares roughly 300 metres across. You claim a square for your team by being in it and hearing the mesh from it. Teams take squares from each other. The team holding the most squares when a season ends wins.

How scoring works
  • You claim a square by hearing repeaters from it. Each repeater a ping hears is worth a tenth of a point, up to one point per ping.
  • A ping that hears no repeaters scores nothing and claims nothing. Being somewhere is not enough — you have to reach the mesh.
  • The first team to paint an unclaimed square takes it.
  • A square that has just been captured cannot be taken for fifteen minutes, no matter what.
  • After that it falls to whoever out-scores the team currently holding it. Only the holder's score matters, not every other team's.
  • The first time a new person paints a square for their team, it is worth an extra half point. More people beats one person going back and forth.
  • The same person cannot repaint the same square within five minutes.
  • Scores fade over time, so held ground has to be revisited.
The two protocols
MeshCore

Playable now. Your position comes from the MeshMapper wardriving app, which forwards a copy of your wardrive to MeshWars.

Meshtastic

The Meshtastic board currently runs on the older system, where nodes are picked up automatically from public packet data rather than by registering. Registering a Meshtastic node now binds it, ready for when that changes over.

How to join
  1. Get the invite code from whoever invited you.
  2. Register at /join, pick a name and a team colour.
  3. Save the API key you are given. It is shown once.
  4. Put the endpoint and key into MeshMapper under Settings, then API Endpoints.
  5. Start a wardriving session and drive.

This does not replace MeshMapper — your standing there is unaffected.

You need an active wardriving session running for anything to be sent.

"Include Contact Key" must stay on.
